UNDERSTANDING YOUR QUOTE

What shapes the cost?

A little detail upfront helps the team understand the work and prepare a quote that reflects it.

01

Contents & size

The amount of furniture, boxes and equipment, including heavy or fragile items.

02

Access & parking

Floors, lifts, stairs, loading bays, parking restrictions and distance to the vehicle.

03

Distance & timing

Collection and delivery locations, your preferred dates and any scheduling requirements.

04

Additional help

Packing, materials, furniture assembly, storage duration and specialist handling.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about your quote?

For a question specific to your move, the team can talk through the details.

Contact the team ↗
How much will my move cost?

There is no single price for every move. Contents, access, travel, timing and the services requested all affect the scope. Request a personalised quote with your details.

What is included in my quote?

Ask for a written breakdown of the agreed work, including packing, materials, dismantling, storage and delivery where relevant.

Can packing or storage be added?

Yes, you can request packing, materials and storage as part of your enquiry. The team can confirm the available options and their cost in your quote.

Is the quote a fixed price?

Ask the team whether the quote is fixed for the agreed scope and what changes to access, contents or dates could affect it.

What about insurance, payment or cancellation?

Confirm the applicable cover, limits and exclusions, payment terms and cancellation conditions directly with the team before booking.
